## THE GOAL

Jon built un-claude.com — a live, working consumer tool that removes AI
watermarks from text and files. He wants press coverage. This task finds
everyone who has ever covered the AI-watermarking story and works out
what each of them actually cares about.

## READ FIRST, FOR THE CLAIMS BOUNDARY

Two files will be provided alongside this task:
  - product-marketing.md — the positioning
  - unclaude-messaging SKILL.md — what may and may not be claimed

**Read the messaging file properly.** The product has three layers and
only two of them are provable. Invisible characters and file metadata are
deterministic and verifiable. **The statistical watermark layer is best
effort and cannot be verified by anyone**, and the site says so plainly.
When you write the "why this would interest them" notes, do not describe
the product in terms it does not claim about itself. Overclaiming to a
technical journalist is how this backfires — they test it and the story
becomes the overclaim.

## CAST A WIDE NET. Jon's instruction, explicitly.

Anyone who has touched this story in any way qualifies. Tiers to cover:

  - **Mainstream news** — NYT, ABC, CNN, BBC, WaPo, Guardian, Reuters, AP
  - **Tech press** — The Verge, Ars Technica, Wired, TechCrunch, 404 Media,
    Rest of World, The Information, Platformer
  - **AI-specific** — VentureBeat AI, MIT Tech Review, IEEE Spectrum,

  - **Academic and research** — anyone who wrote about watermarking papers

  - **Small blogs, Substacks and newsletters** — these often reply when
    large outlets do not. Do not skip them for being small.

## CONTACT DISCIPLINE — this is a hard rule

**Published press contacts only.** A `tips@` address, a newsroom or
editorial address, or a work email a journalist publishes in their own
byline or profile for the purpose of being contacted.

**Do NOT** hunt personal addresses, guess address formats, use
email-permutation tools, or pull from scraped or leaked databases. If a
person has no published contact, record "no published contact" and move
on — that is a complete and acceptable answer.

## WHAT JON IS OFFERING, so you can judge fit

An **interview** and an **exclusive first look**. Note per journalist
which is the better fit, and whether they take pitches at all — some
publish their preferences.

## OUTPUT

**One file: `press-outreach-list.md`.** A table sorted by priority, then
short per-person notes for anyone marked high.

Then a short summary: how many found, the tier spread, which angles
produced the most, and **which names you would personally write to first
and why.**

**Say plainly what you could not find** — outlets with no reachable
contact, paywalled articles you could not read, angles that returned
nothing. A gap named is useful; a gap hidden is not.

## SCOPE AND STOPPING

Cast wide, but **quality of the per-person notes beats raw count.** A
list of 200 names with generic notes is worth less than 60 with a real
hook each. If you must choose, choose depth.
