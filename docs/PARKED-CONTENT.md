## The three industry figures, removed 19 August 2026

**Where they were:** beside the live counter, below the tool, as three cards with
a `+` expander each.

**Why they came off.** Jon: *"we need new metrics altogether there. Or we can
scrap that section and maybe it doesn't belong."* Two problems, both his:

- **The expander text is far too long.** Three and four sentences behind a `+`
  that nobody clicks. Not read, so not doing a job.
- **They are facts about an industry, not about the reader.** A visitor who has
  just been told Claude marks their writing does not need to be told the market
  size.

**They are accurate.** Sourced from `ENGINE.md` section 2, researched 18 August
2026, and none is rounded up to sound bigger.

### 5 of 8

**Label:** of the biggest AI companies already mark what they make

**Detail:** Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, Adobe and the hosted Stability and Flux
services all attach a signed record to the files they produce, and they settled
on one shared format for it. Every major lab has also signed the European
transparency code, so the other three are a question of when rather than whether.

### 2.5bn

**Label:** prompts answered by ChatGPT every day. That is one product, at one
company

**Detail:** Reported in 2026. Nobody can read their way through that much
machine-written text to work out where it came from, which is the whole reason
the marks exist and the whole reason they are invisible.

### Aug 2026

**Label:** when Claude started marking the writing it produces

**Detail:** Anthropic applies it to models launched from 2 August 2026,
everywhere, with no way to switch it off. Google already does the same to Gemini.
Neither mark can be checked by any tool the public can use, which is why removing
it is the only option anyone actually has today.

**One caution if this one returns.** The last sentence says no public tool can
check either mark. **That is true and Jon has ruled it should not be shouted on
the front page** — *"why is that screaming at you at the very front of this
website when that's kind of why we're here and we want your payment?"* `04` entry
78. It belongs where a reader has already decided to trust us, not where they are
deciding whether to.
