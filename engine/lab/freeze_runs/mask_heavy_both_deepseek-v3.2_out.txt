The auditor's conclusion was confined to a single paragraph. Her note stated that the panel held "acted without malice and without competence in equal measure, spending eleven months and the whole contingency reserve on a bridge survey that the county had already completed twice, most recently in the spring before the war memorial fund was raided for the deposit."

The minutes recorded, in full:

    No member present could say who had ordered the second survey,
    who had signed for the deposit, or where the first survey was
    filed. The clerk's ledger shows the sum leaving and nothing
    arriving.

No one stepped down in response, and that silence conveyed to the hamlet all it required regarding the outcome of the following review.
