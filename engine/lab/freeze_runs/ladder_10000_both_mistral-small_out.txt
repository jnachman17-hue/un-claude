Smith (2018) distilled the finding into the simplest verbiage available at the time, and this assertion has been cited verbatim in every feasibility study compiled thereafter: "the change in school start times did more for adolescent attendance than any intervention we had previously funded, including the 2012 programme that consumed the whole of the improvement budget" (Smith, 2018, p. 38). The assertion endured scrutiny through two confirmatory analyses and one unsuccessful re-examination within a district that had independently altered its own agenda.

The 2018 evaluation of twenty-three jurisdictions arrived at an identical verdict through an alternate avenue. Its compilers asserted that "the evidence base is thinner than the policy assumes, and thicker than its critics allow" — a statement frequently truncated by partisans on either side of the debate. The dissent merits full quotation, as it is habitually diluted into a milder interpretation. The minority assessment documented:
