The entrance to the barn had stayed agape since sunrise, yet nobody would explain why. Ruth verified the canines' count on two occasions and was still a single pup missing both efforts.

She declared, "We cannot linger another evening," while scanning the roadway. "They're aware the bridge is impassable and aware that we're aware too."

Marcus stated, "Then we'll depart at nightfall," already unfurling the canvas sheet. "You steer the dogs along the distant pasture. I'll drive the cart behind you with lamps extinguished."

Ruth queried, "What if the current covers the fording point?" She'd inspected it at midday and rocks had been submerged even then.

He answered, "Then we endure the damp," which encapsulated the entire methodology - conditions paramount, apprehensions secondary - identical to his longstanding approach.

She ascended to the hayloft to reclaim the final sack of seed potatoes and paused briefly beside the small aperture. The thoroughfare stretched clear toward the elm trees at the horizon. Whatever seized the hound evidently spared the geese, a detail implying something bipedal, though she'd long dismissed notions of itinerant beings when the span collapsed.
